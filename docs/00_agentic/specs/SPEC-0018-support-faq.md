# SPEC-0018 — Support FAQ（英文内容）

## Q1: Why can't I connect?
A: Please switch to another location and try again. If the problem persists, check your network or try later.

## Q2: Why is the speed slow?
A: Speed depends on your network and server distance. Choose the "Fastest" location for better performance.

## Q3: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings. Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

## Q4: How do I restore purchases?
A: Tap "Restore Purchases" on the paywall to restore your subscription.
